Watches Fanatics Collect Buy Now listings and alerts when buying one and relisting at your resale price clears your minimum profit.

    Est. profit = resale price × (1 − resale fee) − Buy Now price. Min profit filters Buy Now alerts; Best Offer alerts still come through, showing profit at the asking price.

    eBay watcher

    Fanatics flipper

    Fanatics Buy Now charges sellers 6% when listed under 120% of Card Ladder value, 12% at or above it, and 0% with a FanCash payout. The resale fee comes off the resale price when estimating profit.

    Intervals are 15 seconds minimum; a change applies after the current wait.

    Shipping ZIP

    eBay only quotes calculated shipping for a delivery address. Alert totals include shipping to this ZIP.

    eBay account deletion endpoint

    eBay requires this before production API keys work. Paste the public URL of /ebay/notifications, generate a token, save, then register both on developer.ebay.com/my/keys.

    Card index

    Card search uses TCGplayer's product list from the daily tcgcsv.com dumps. The index builds automatically on startup and refreshes daily so new sets show up in card search; each build takes a few minutes.
